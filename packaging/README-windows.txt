Ultimate Grapple (Windows, x86_64)

Run UltimateGrapple.exe.
Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown app (it isn't code-signed):
click "More info" -> "Run anyway".

Older GPUs without Vulkan: make a shortcut with
  UltimateGrapple.exe --rendering-driver opengl3

Saves, replays and settings: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Ultimate Grapple
MP4 replay export needs ffmpeg on your PATH.
Newest build: https://github.com/malleum/ultimategrapple/releases/latest
